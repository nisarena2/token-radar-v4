How the value is worked out

Age is shown but never priced: an age factor was tested on the time split and dropped — it did not improve out-of-sample misses. A band, not a point: every implied value carries its P25–P75 range from the leave-one-out residuals, because a launch value is a range, not a promise. Nothing here is a hardcoded list of names: the curve, the category effects and the verdicts are all computed from this snapshot's own figures.

Farm verdicts are netted to dollars on a $1,000 plan. Slice = your $1,000 share of TVL × implied value × the community's 10% share of a launch — airdrops typically put about 10% of supply to the community, so a farmer's slice is a pro-rata share of that 10%, not of the whole value. Costs: $3 gas per chain the plan actually farms (the main chain and one more, at most 2), 0.3% protocol fees, and 4% a year of forgone T-bill yield over a 90-day farm. FARM clears $10 net and $5 per hands-on hour; THIN clears $0; the rest is SKIP.
